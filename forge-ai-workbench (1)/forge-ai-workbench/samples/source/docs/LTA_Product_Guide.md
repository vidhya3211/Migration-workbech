# Level Term Assurance - Product Guide (extract)

## Premiums
A monthly administration charge of GBP 25.00 is added to every premium.
Policyholders aged 60 or over at the calculation date attract an age loading of 25%.
Smokers pay 50% more than non-smokers.

## Surrender
Surrenders in the first three policy years incur a 5% penalty on fund value; years
three and four incur 3%. A fixed exit fee of GBP 50.00 applies.
